Hermes Personal

A personal Google-account integration operated by Alastair Chaffin for his own locally operated Hermes automation. This is not an official Nous Research service and is not open for public registration.

Purpose

The integration connects Google services authorized by the account owner, including Gmail, Calendar, Tasks, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts, to assist with owner-requested tasks. The permissions actually requested are shown on Google's authorization screen. Access is limited by those granted permissions; actions depend on the owner's instructions and configuration.

This website only provides public application information. It does not sign users in or process Google account data.

Data handling

Depending on the task and configured providers, selected Google data may appear in AI model requests, automation logs, or chat responses. Read the privacy information before authorizing the integration.
