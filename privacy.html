Privacy information

This information describes Alastair Chaffin's personal Google-account integration, Hermes Personal. It is not a public service or an official Nous Research service.

Information accessed and purposes

With the account owner's authorization, the integration can access Google service data within the permissions granted on Google's consent screen. Depending on those permissions and the requested task, this can include email and attachments, calendar events, tasks, files and document or spreadsheet contents, and contacts. Data is used to carry out owner-requested personal automation, such as finding information, preparing responses, and reading or updating authorized records.

Processing and disclosure

Automation runs in the owner's configured Hermes environment. Selected Google data may be sent to configured AI model or other service providers as needed for a task, and may be included in chat responses, local records, or logs. Provider handling, retention, and any model-training use depend on the selected providers and account settings; this page does not promise that third-party processing or training never occurs.

This personal connector does not implement data sales or advertising. The public application-information website does not contain Google account data and adds no analytics scripts or cookies. GitHub hosts the website and may process ordinary web-request information under its own policies.

Retention and control

The account owner manages locally retained credentials, logs, chat history, and other records. No fixed automatic deletion schedule is represented here. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically remove previously retained local or provider records. Local copies and Google originals are separate; deleting one does not necessarily delete the other.

The owner can revoke the integration through Google Account third-party connections and can separately manage or delete retained records in the configured local environment and provider accounts. Never publish access tokens, credentials, or private account content in this repository or its issues.

Contact

Contact the operator through GitHub issues (public; do not post personal information, Google data, credentials, or tokens).
