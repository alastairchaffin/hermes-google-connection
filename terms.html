Usage information and terms

Hermes Personal is operated by Alastair Chaffin solely for owner-authorized personal Google-account automation. It is not a public service, is not open for registration, and is not an official Nous Research offering.

Authorized use

Use is limited to the account owner and activities the owner is authorized to perform. Google authorization grants the selected permissions, not permission to access another person's account or act beyond the owner's authority. Do not publish credentials or private account data.

Review important outputs and actions

Automation and AI-generated outputs may be incomplete or incorrect. The owner should verify important outputs and review consequential actions, including messages, edits, deletions, and scheduling changes. No service availability or accuracy guarantee is offered by this personal project.

Stopping use

The owner may stop running the integration and revoke its Google access at any time through Google Account third-party connections. Previously retained local or provider records must be managed separately, as described in the privacy information.

These are practical usage notes for a personal integration, not a separately negotiated legal contract or an offer of a public service. Questions may be raised through GitHub issues (public; do not post personal information, Google data, credentials, or tokens).
